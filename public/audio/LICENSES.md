# Audio asset licenses

The audio files in this directory are redistributed separately from the Swara Flow source code.

## Flute samples

Files: `flute/C4.wav`, `flute/G4.wav`, `flute/C5.wav`, `flute/G5.wav`

- Source: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples
- Performer: Sonja Feig
- Recording: non-vibrato concert flute, fortissimo, stereo
- Original files: `Flute.nonvib.ff.C4.stereo.aif`, `G4`, `C5`, and `G5`
- Source page: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS-Pitches-2012/MISFlute2012.html
- Usage statement: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
- Terms stated by the source: recordings may be downloaded and used for any projects without restrictions.
- Modification: converted from AIFF to mono 16-bit WAV for browser delivery.
